Sue Wheeler has been refinishing architectural woodwork in St. Louis since 1989. In that time, she's restored original pocket doors in Central West End bungalows, refinished the woodwork at The Sheldon Concert Hall, matched 100-year-old stain on built-in hutches in Compton Heights, and brought back hundreds of kitchen cabinets that homeowners thought were past saving.

Wood refinishing photographs badly if you're not careful about light. Most of our best work — a perfectly matched stain on a 90-year-old built-in, a front door that looks like it was installed yesterday — doesn't look dramatic under overhead fluorescents.

We're adding to this gallery steadily. If you want to see work that matches your specific situation — a particular wood species, finish type, or level of distress — call Sue. She can describe what she's seen and what to expect. That conversation is free, and it's more useful than a photo.
