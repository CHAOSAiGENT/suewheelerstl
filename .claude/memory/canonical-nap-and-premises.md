Canonical NAP for all listings, schema, and citations — decided 2026-08-29:

- **Name:** Wood Refinishing by Sue Wheeler, LLC (legal name; already used in all 63 `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD nodes)
- **Address:** 900 S Boyle Ave, St. Louis, MO 63110
- **Phone:** (314) 367-6054 / +13143676054 (already consistent everywhere)

**Premises classification is HYBRID, not service-area-only.** Customers _can_ come to the 900 S Boyle workshop to drop off and collect items, even though most work happens on-site at the customer's home and drop-off is not the promoted path. This matters: a hybrid earns a proximity-based local-pack position that a pure service-area business does not. The address should be SHOWN on the Google Business Profile, with service areas layered on top — do not hide it.

**Two live complications:**

1. **5260 Washington Pl, 63108 — the address currently on the Google Business Profile — is Sue's home.** Verified against property records: a 1907 single-family house. It is published as the business address on BBB, Yelp, Yellow Pages, and Yahoo Local. Every third-party citation carries it; **zero** carry Boyle. This is a privacy exposure first and a NAP problem second, and the directory cleanup requires Sue's logins.
2. **900 S Boyle is a shared 21,000 sq ft warehouse.** _The City Cages_ batting cages operates at the same street address with its own Google listing. Sue's GBP needs a **suite/unit designator**, and the address change should use **video verification** — it shows the real workshop and resolves the same-address collision. Expect re-verification and a short ranking wobble.

Decision: ONE Google Business Profile, never two. Multiple listings at different addresses split ranking authority and reviews, and Google only permits them for genuinely distinct staffed customer-facing locations.
